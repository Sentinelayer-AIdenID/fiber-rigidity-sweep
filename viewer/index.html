Each fit uses at least five converged, positive K′ samples above the chosen onset, with complete seed stencils. Default distance window: 0.05γc ≤ γ − γc ≤ 0.5γc, spanning at least a factor of three. Uncertainty is conditional on the onset and window; it excludes onset, seed and finite-size uncertainty. Sharma et al. (2016) report f ≈ 0.7 for the strain-controlled class; this is a comparison, not a fit constraint. Hover over a fitted exponent for its conditional 95% interval.

A map, not a universal curve. The bundled small CPU run demonstrates the workflow. Finite size, the strain grid, the bending stiffness and incomplete relaxation can shift or obscure a transition. Read the README for the model and fitting conventions.
